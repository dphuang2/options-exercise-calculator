# Stock Options Exercise Calculator - California 2025

A web-based calculator to help you determine the maximum number of stock options you can exercise without triggering Alternative Minimum Tax (AMT) in California for 2025.

## Features

- **AMT Avoidance**: Calculates the maximum number of shares you can exercise without triggering AMT
- **2025 Tax Rates**: Uses accurate 2025 Federal and California tax brackets
- **Multiple Scenarios**: Shows various exercise scenarios with tax implications
- **Visual Indicators**: Highlights scenarios that trigger AMT vs. those that don't
- **Comprehensive Breakdown**: Detailed tax breakdown for the recommended exercise amount

## How to Use

1. Open `index.html` in your web browser
2. Enter your options details:
   - **Total Options in Grant**: Total number of options you have
   - **Exercise Price per Share**: The strike price of your options
   - **Fair Market Value (FMV) per Share**: Current fair market value of the stock
   - **Expected Gross Salary 2025**: Your expected salary for 2025
   - **Filing Status**: Single, Married Filing Jointly, or Head of Household
3. Click "Calculate"
4. Review the results:
   - The calculator shows the maximum number of shares you can exercise without triggering AMT
   - Scenarios table shows different exercise amounts with their tax implications
   - Rows with ⚠️ indicate AMT would be triggered
   - The recommended row (⭐) shows the maximum without AMT

## Tax Information Used

### Federal Tax Brackets (2025)
- Standard Deduction: $15,000 (Single), $30,000 (Married), $22,500 (Head of Household)
- Progressive brackets from 10% to 37%

### California Tax Brackets (2025)
- Standard Deduction: $5,706 (Single), $11,412 (Married)
- Progressive brackets from 1% to 12.3%

### AMT (Alternative Minimum Tax)
- Exemption: $73,600 (Single), $114,600 (Married)
- Rate: 26% on income up to $199,900, 28% above
- AMT is triggered when AMT tax exceeds regular federal tax

## Important Notes

- This calculator assumes **Incentive Stock Options (ISOs)**, which can trigger AMT
- The calculator provides estimates only - tax laws are complex and subject to change
- **Always consult with a tax professional** for personalized advice
- AMT calculations are simplified - actual AMT may have additional factors

## Disclaimer

This calculator is for educational and estimation purposes only. Tax laws are complex and individual circumstances vary. Always consult with a qualified tax professional before making decisions about exercising stock options.
